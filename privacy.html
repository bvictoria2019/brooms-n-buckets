Privacy

What we keep.

Booking and contact forms collect the details you type so we can answer and schedule. That includes your name, phone, email, address, and notes about the home.

A Customer Portal account keeps your name, phone, email, and a password hash so you can sign in and see your own visits. We do not keep the password itself.

A review shows your first name, neighborhood, star choice, and the words you wrote. The email on that form is not shown on the page.

We do not sell this information. Write scheduler@brooms-n-buckets.com if you want a review taken down or a request deleted.
