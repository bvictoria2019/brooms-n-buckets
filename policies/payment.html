House rules

Payment

Payment is due as described here. The details for Zelle or Venmo are given when you book.

Ways to pay

  • Zelle
  • Venmo
  • Cash
  • Check

When payment is due

  • A 50% deposit is due at booking for new customers and for deep cleans. See booking and cancellation.
  • The remaining balance is due when the service is finished.
  • Recurring clients pay on the day of service, unless we have arranged something else.

Deposit

The deposit is not refundable. It may be moved once, to a new date within 14 days of the original appointment.

Returned or late payment

  • A returned check or a declined electronic payment has a $25 fee. Future visits may require the full amount before we come.
  • Payment not received within 24 hours of the completed service has a $25 late fee.
  • Repeated late payment can pause service until the account is current.

An unpaid balance

A new appointment is not scheduled while a balance is open. Service stays paused until it is paid.

Receipts

Receipts are available on request. Invoices can be provided for HSA or FSA reimbursement when the plan allows it.
