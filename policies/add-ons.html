Policies

Add-on services

Add-ons customize a scheduled clean. They are not offered by themselves. Priced add-ons are on the rate sheet.

What you can ask for

  • Oven cleaning, including the interior and racks
  • Refrigerator cleaning, when the fridge is empty
  • Interior windows, tracks, and sills
  • Baseboards
  • Dusting and wiping of reachable light fixtures
  • Dishes washed and put away, or loaded in the dishwasher
  • Inside cabinets, when they are empty
  • Decluttering or organizing a named area, such as a closet or pantry
  • Extra attention for heavy pet hair
  • Another request, if we agree it fits the visit

Ask ahead

  • Request add-ons when you book, or at least 24 hours before the appointment.
  • They depend on the calendar and the size of the job.
  • A same-day request may not be possible.

Price

The price depends on the service, the home, and how much detail you want. We quote add-ons when you book. Some take extra time and change the total. A heavily soiled area can cost more. Windows and a short-term rental reset are priced on the rate sheet. The short-term rental visit is a reset only.
